Privacy

This site answers one question and keeps almost nothing about you while doing it. The one exception is the advertising, which is third-party code; this page explains exactly what it does.

What stays on your device

Your location, calculation method, ruling basis, and display preferences are stored in your browser's local storage under cipn.* keys. They never leave your device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Location

If you use "Use my location", the coordinates are read by your browser and used locally to compute prayer windows - they are not sent to us. The fallback /api/geo endpoint returns the approximate city Cloudflare already derives from the connection; we do not store your IP address or any identifier.

What we collect

We run no first-party analytics: no account, no first-party analytics cookies, and we build no profile of you ourselves. We do not know who you are, and your location, ruling basis, and settings are never sent anywhere. If a sponsor card is shown it is a static, clearly labelled card served from this site; clicking it passes through a counter that records only that a click happened - no cookies, no user IDs. The one place cookies and third-party code enter is the advertising, described next.

Advertising

The site carries advertising from the Muslim Ad Network, a halal-focused ad network. Showing an ad loads a script from the network's servers, which is the one piece of third-party code on the page. Like most ad networks, that script and the ads it serves can set their own cookies and measure whether an ad was actually seen, in order to deliver and count ads; your location, your ruling basis, and your settings are never shared with it. Ads load only after your answer is on screen, sit below the content so they can never cover the verdict, and a kill-switch lets us remove them instantly.

To publish with the network we also verify the site using its Google (DoubleClick) tag. That tag sets a Google advertising cookie and confirms the site and its ad activity to the network. You can limit or clear these ad cookies through your browser's site-data controls or Google's Ads Settings. If you would rather none of it were there, we understand; the advertising is what keeps the tool free.

Affiliate links

Some sponsor cards are affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases: if you buy through one, we may receive a small commission at no extra cost to you. This changes nothing about what we store about you.

Offline

The app caches its own files so it works offline. All prayer-time computation happens in your browser.
