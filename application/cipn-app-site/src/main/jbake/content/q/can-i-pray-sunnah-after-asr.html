Can I pray sunnah after Asr?

Ḥanafī: no. From the moment Asr is prayed (the app anchors conservatively to Asr's entry) no voluntary prayer is begun until Maghrib; doing so is makrūh taḥrīmī, to be avoided. Shāfiʿī: it depends on the cause. A sunnah tied to a present cause, such as greeting the mosque or the two rakʿahs after wuḍūʾ, remains valid after Asr; a pure voluntary prayer with no cause does not. Sources

These rulings are grounded in the Qurʾān and authentic Sunnah:

  • No voluntary prayer after Fajr or Asr - Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī: Bukhārī 586, Muslim 827.
  • The Prophet made up two rakʿahs after Asr - Umm Salamah: Bukhārī 1233, Muslim 834.
