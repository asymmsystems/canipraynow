Can I pray after Fajr?

It depends what you mean to pray. Between the entry of Fajr and sunrise you pray the Fajr prayer itself, its two rakʿahs of sunnah, any missed prayers (in the Ḥanafī view), and the funeral prayer. What is withheld is other voluntary prayer: from the entry of Fajr, nafl is suspended until the sun has not only risen but climbed for about twenty minutes. In the Shāfiʿī school, voluntary prayers that have a specific cause, such as greeting the mosque, remain valid even then. The answer below is conditioned on exactly what you intend to pray.

Sources

These rulings are grounded in the Qurʾān and authentic Sunnah:

  • No voluntary prayer after Fajr or Asr - Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī: Bukhārī 586, Muslim 827.
  • A missed prayer is prayed when remembered - Anas ibn Mālik: Bukhārī 597, Muslim 684.
  • Two rakʿahs on entering during the Friday khutbah - Jābir: Bukhārī 931, Muslim 875.
