# Prayer-Time Method Fact-Check — UAE / Kuwait / Qatar

Verified reference values (api.aladhan.com/v1/methods, opened directly): Dubai 18.2/18.2;
Gulf Region 19.5°/90 min; Kuwait 18/17.5; Qatar 18/90 min; Umm al-Qura 18.5/90 min.

## UAE — assigned "Dubai" preset (18.2/18.2) — NO / OVERSIMPLIFIED

The "Dubai (UAE)" method in aladhan (18.2/18.2) is NOT an official government method — it comes
from third-party Batoul Apps research (an angle-fit approximation). The real UAE federal authority
is GAIAE / Awqaf, and its official method is effectively the Gulf Region method: Fajr 19.5° and
Isha = 90 minutes after Maghrib (fixed interval). Same as Dubai's IACAD. Difference between the
18.2 preset and the official table runs ~1–5 min per prayer. (arabeyes ITL Method 7 = UAE/QA/BH/OM:
Fajr 19.5°, Isha 90 min.)

## Kuwait — assigned 18/17.5 — YES

aladhan Kuwait = Fajr 18° / Isha 17.5° (both angle-based). No fixed-table deviation.

## Qatar — assigned 18/90 min — YES (caveat)

aladhan Qatar = Fajr 18° / Isha 90 min after Maghrib — exactly the preset. Caveat: Qatar Calendar
House is sometimes described as Umm al-Qura-based (Fajr 18.5°); Isha 90 min consistent everywhere.
qatarcalendarhouse.com did not resolve (DNS) — relied on aladhan + secondary sources.

## Summary

| Country | Assigned | Matches? | Authority's actual method |
|---|---|---|---|
| UAE | Dubai 18.2/18.2 | NO / OVERSIMPLIFIED | GAIAE/Awqaf: Fajr 19.5°, Isha 90 min. 18.2 is unofficial Batoul-Apps fit. |
| Kuwait | 18/17.5 | YES | Fajr 18°, Isha 17.5° |
| Qatar | 18/90 min | YES (Fajr may be 18.5°) | Fajr 18° (or 18.5°), Isha 90 min |

Bottom line: only UAE is wrong — should use Gulf Region (19.5° + 90-min), what GAIAE/Awqaf and IACAD publish.
