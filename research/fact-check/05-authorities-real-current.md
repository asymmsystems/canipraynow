# Prayer-time authority verification (real / current / correctly named)

Spot-check that each named national authority exists, is current, correctly named, and actually
sets prayer times (not a moon-sighting-only body).

- **ACJU — All Ceylon Jamiyyathul Ulama (Sri Lanka)** — REAL & CORRECTLY NAMED. acju.lk/prayer-times. Incorporated by Act 51/2000; official district timetables (monthly PDF/image).
- **JAKIM — Jabatan Kemajuan Islam Malaysia / e-Solat** — REAL & CORRECTLY NAMED. e-solat.gov.my. ~59–68 zones. Sets Malaysia's official times.
- **MUIS — Majlis Ugama Islam Singapura** — REAL & CORRECTLY NAMED. muis.gov.sg. Statutory board under AMLA; sole authority for Singapore times.
- **Kemenag / Bimas Islam (Indonesia)** — REAL & CORRECTLY NAMED. bimasislam.kemenag.go.id/jadwalshalat. Official jadwal salat by province/city.
- **Diyanet — Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı (Turkey)** — REAL & CORRECTLY NAMED. namazvakitleri.diyanet.gov.tr.
- **Qatar Calendar House (Dar Al-Taqweem Al-Qatari)** — REAL & CORRECTLY NAMED. qatarch.com. Astronomy/calendar dept publishing Qatar's official times.
- **"Egyptian General Authority of Survey"** — MISNAMED. Real body: **Egyptian Survey Authority (ESA)**, الهيئة المصرية العامة للمساحة ("Egyptian General Authority for Surveying"), under the Ministry of Water Resources & Irrigation (mwri.gov.eg). A state surveying agency historically credited with the 19.5/17.5 parameters — NOT a religious council. Do not conflate with NARSS.

## Bottom line

Authorities 1–6 are real and correctly named. Only #7 needs a fix — use "Egyptian Survey Authority" and note it is a state surveying agency.
