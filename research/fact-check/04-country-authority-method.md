# Prayer-time AUTHORITY + METHOD fact-check — findings

Per-country check: is the named national authority real and correctly named, and is the method
the app assigns the one that authority actually uses? Verified against the aladhan method
registry, the arabeyes ITL method-info doc, and each authority's own site.

## Bottom line

- **Actually wrong:** Azerbaijan → Tehran (imports Shia delayed-Maghrib; use MWL); Malaysia labeled "MUIS/Singapore" (authority is JAKIM; times unchanged).
- **Four flagged corrections hold up** (Sub-Saharan Africa, France, Russia, Levant), with Jordan's own 18/18 Awqaf method the softest spot.
- **Spot-check authorities all real** — ACJU, JAKIM, MUIS, Kemenag/Bimas Islam, Diyanet, Qatar Calendar House. Only naming slips: Malaysia (MUIS→JAKIM) and Egypt (→"Egyptian Survey Authority").
- **Oversimplified-but-disclosed** (table deviates): UAE, Morocco, Bangladesh, Balkans, Nordics, Turkey, Indonesia; add Qatar and Central Asia.
