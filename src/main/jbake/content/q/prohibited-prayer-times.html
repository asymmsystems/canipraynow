Prohibited prayer times (awqāt al-nahy)

Five windows matter. Three are strict, tied to the sun itself: sunrise (from the sun's first appearance until it has fully risen, conventionally about twenty minutes), the zenith (the few minutes the sun stands at its highest, until zawāl passes), and sunset (from the sun's yellowing, isfirār, until Maghrib). Two are extended: after the entry of Fajr and after Asr, in which voluntary prayer is withheld but obligatory, missed, and funeral prayers have their own rulings depending on the school. These windows move every day with the sun, which is why a fixed timetable cannot answer "can I pray right now" - the tool below computes them astronomically for your coordinates.

Sources

These rulings are grounded in the Qurʾān and authentic Sunnah:

  • The three sun-times (prayer and burial withheld) - ʿUqbah ibn ʿĀmir: Muslim 831.
  • No voluntary prayer after Fajr or Asr - Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī: Bukhārī 586, Muslim 827.
  • Qurʾān, an-Nisāʾ 4:103 - "prayer has been decreed upon the believers a decree of specified times".
